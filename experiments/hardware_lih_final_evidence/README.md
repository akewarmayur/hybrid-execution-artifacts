# Final hardware evidence campaign

This directory is the isolated namespace for
`hardware_lih_sigmetrics_2027_final_evidence_campaign`.

The campaign independently qualifies up to five 5q backends, evaluates every
qualified backend as a source, optionally qualifies and evaluates up to three
7q backends, and finally runs historical-envelope diagnostics if budget
remains. Its new-campaign provider-QPU budget is fixed at 2700 seconds.

The two predecessor qualification studies are read-only provenance inputs.
Neither is described as a successful final hardware evaluation.
